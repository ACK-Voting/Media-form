# History page photographs — provenance

Every photograph on `/history` was extracted from a booklet held by the
Cathedral, cropped from a 300 DPI scan, and uploaded to Cloudinary under
`ack/history`. This table records exactly which page each one came from.

**Why this file exists.** *Who Built Mombasa's Anglican Cathedral?* (Canon Dr.
Steve Foster KStG, © canonprint–mmc–2021) thanks a list of archives for data
access — among them the Alamy Pictorial Archive, the *Illustrated London News*
Archive, the National Archive in Nairobi, the Foreign and Commonwealth Office
and Ministry of Defence archives, Canterbury Cathedral, the Royal Academy, the
Royal Navy personnel records, John Warner and Company, University College
Oxford and the Wray Collection. The booklet does **not** say which archive
supplied which picture, so that mapping cannot be worked out from the document
and has to come from its author.

**Before launch,** please ask Canon Foster to confirm the source of each
historic image below, and in particular whether any came from Alamy — Alamy is
a commercial stock agency and licenses images for publication.

The modern colour photographs from the Welcome Visitor Book are the Cathedral's
own pictures of its own building and raise no such question.

Images sit in Cloudinary at:
`https://res.cloudinary.com/dcuymhwnp/image/upload/ack/history/<name>.jpg`

All 51 are uploaded; 33 are placed on the page today. The rest are in the
library for staff to use from `/cms/history` — nothing needs re-extracting.

## Two captions to check

- **`visitors-1909`** — the Welcome Visitor Book captions this "Sir James
  Sadler-Governor, Bishop William Peel (civilian dress), USA President Theodore
  Roosevelt, Sir Winston Churchill". Roosevelt had left office in March 1909 and
  was travelling as a *former* president; Churchill's East African tour was in
  **1907**, not 1909. The image is uploaded but **deliberately not placed on the
  page** until the caption is settled. Staff can add it from `/cms/history` once
  it is.
- **`lady-elliot`** — the source scan is very faded. It is included because it is
  the only known likeness, but it may be worth rescanning the original.
